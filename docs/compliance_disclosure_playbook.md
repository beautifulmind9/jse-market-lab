# Compliance and Disclosure Playbook — JSE Market Lab

## Purpose

This playbook defines product-language and disclosure boundaries for JSE Market Lab as it expands from a decision-support dashboard into a broader market intelligence, research, education, brokerage-access, and monetization platform.

This is an internal planning document. Final public language should be reviewed by qualified legal/compliance professionals and relevant partner institutions before launch.

---

## Platform Boundary

JSE Market Lab provides:
- educational market context
- historical market analysis
- decision-support tools
- research discovery
- company context
- portfolio planning support
- trade readiness context
- account-opening education
- referral/routing workflows to licensed institutions, if approved

JSE Market Lab does not:
- provide personal investment advice
- recommend buy/sell actions
- guarantee outcomes
- predict future prices
- execute trades
- open brokerage accounts directly
- hold client funds
- act as a broker-dealer

---

## Required Decision-Support Language

Allowed wording:
- The dashboard shows...
- The system flagged...
- This setup appears stronger/weaker based on the current rules...
- Review this before acting...
- This is a decision-support view, not a prediction...
- The data is incomplete, so confidence should be lower...
- This trade was not funded because...
- Use this as a review point before deciding whether it fits your own risk tolerance and plan.

Avoid wording:
- Buy this stock
- Sell this stock
- This will go up
- This is guaranteed
- You should invest
- This is risk-free
- The AI recommends...
- This stock is definitely a winner
- Ignore the warning

---

## Broker Referral / Account-Opening Disclosures

Any brokerage-access flow must clearly state:
- JSE Market Lab does not open brokerage accounts
- JSE Market Lab does not execute trades
- JSE Market Lab does not hold funds
- account opening is handled by a licensed financial institution
- the user should review all fees, risks, requirements, and documents directly with the institution
- JSE Market Lab may receive a referral fee if applicable and if approved/allowed
- sponsored or paid broker placement must be disclosed

Required consent language:

> By submitting this form, you agree that JSE Market Lab may share your contact details and account-opening interest with a licensed financial institution for follow-up. JSE Market Lab does not provide investment advice, open accounts, hold funds, or execute trades.

Broker CTA allowed language:
- Need a brokerage account to invest on the JSE?
- Start account-opening guide
- Connect with a licensed institution
- Learn what documents may be required

Broker CTA forbidden language:
- Best broker
- Recommended broker
- Buy now
- Invest in this stock
- This broker will give you the best return

---

## Sponsored Content Disclosures

Any paid placement, sponsored content, broker-funded placement, research distribution arrangement, or institution-facing package must clearly disclose:
- whether content is sponsored
- who sponsored it
- whether a broker paid for placement
- whether a referral fee may be earned
- whether research is original, partner-provided, licensed, or linked only

Disclosure should appear near the relevant content, not only in footer language.

---

## Research Hosting Rules

Hosting policy:

> Index and summarize responsibly. Host only where permission or licensing exists.

Rights statuses:
- public_link_only
- permission_to_host
- partner_research
- original_jse_market_lab_summary
- licensed_content
- do_not_host

Rules:
- Do not upload or host full third-party reports unless permission/licensing exists.
- Public pages may link to public source URLs.
- Summaries should be original and not copied extensively from source documents.
- Source, date, and rights status should be visible internally.
- User-facing summaries should identify where the source came from.

## Analyst Scoreboard Disclosures

The Analyst Accountability feature should begin as a Research Call Tracker.

Allowed language:
- Call history
- Research performance
- Documented outcomes
- Scored calls
- Tracking in progress
- Methodology

Avoid language:
- Worst analyst
- Bad analyst
- Exposed
- Failed prediction

Required disclosures:
- scoring methodology
- time horizon used
- benchmark used
- whether dividend/corporate-action adjustments were applied
- whether liquidity adjustments were applied
- minimum sample size for leaderboard eligibility
- correction/dispute process

Minimum leaderboard rule:
- public leaderboard eligibility requires at least 10 scored calls

---

## AI Output Guardrails

The AI layer should explain structured outputs. It should not invent market opinions or give advice.

AI may help with:
- data quality explanations
- signal explanations
- portfolio rationale
- trade readiness summaries
- event/news risk context
- weekly market insights

AI must not:
- say buy/sell
- predict price movement
- guarantee returns
- rank stocks as sure winners
- hide missing data
- make recommendations outside dashboard rules
- invent unsupported facts

Every AI-assisted feature must include tests for:
- grounding
- required facts
- missing-data disclosure
- safe financial language
- beginner clarity
- analyst usefulness
- tone

## Complaint / Escalation Process

Future operating process should define:
1. User complaint received
2. Complaint categorized
3. Owner assigned
4. Source evidence reviewed
5. Correction or response documented
6. User/institution notified where appropriate
7. Product copy or logic updated if needed

Complaint categories:
- data error
- misleading explanation
- broker/referral issue
- research rights issue
- analyst scoring dispute
- AI output concern

## Validation Needed Before Launch

### With JSE
- data usage rights
- redistribution rights
- API/data feed options
- whether public summaries derived from JSE data are allowed
- branding/naming constraints

### With FSC / qualified counsel
- investment advice boundaries
- broker referral fee permissibility
- account-opening lead routing
- research hosting and scoring
- analyst leaderboard risks
- sponsored content disclosures
- requirements for diaspora / non-resident investor flows

### With broker partners
- accepted investor jurisdictions
- remote onboarding process
- minimum funding
- referral structure
- compliance approval for copy
- complaint/escalation process

---

## Product Rule

When in doubt, use clearer disclosure and weaker claims.

The product should win trust by being transparent about what it knows, what it does not know, what is derived, what is sponsored, and what requires a licensed institution.
